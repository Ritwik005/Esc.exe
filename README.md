# Esc.exe
## A Game Designed for Computer Graphics Project
### Created By: ***Ritwik Pathak***

### Make sure to read this before playing!

Building 6A, home to the psychedelic ward where experimental procedures were conducted, appears to have been overtaken by 'Them'. You were sent to investigate what is happening inside the ward.

Now you’re trapped.

Lose your sanity, and it’s over.

Your only objective: **Escape**.
<br>
<br>
<br>
## Tutorial:
🟥 Red Bar represents your health. Do not **die** out there.

🟦 Blue Bar represents your stamina. Yes, it can deplete.

🟩 Green Bar represents your sanity. Lose your sanity and it's over.

🟨 Yellow Bar represents the battery health for your flashlight. Make sure to escape before you lose your vision.

Your Objectives Include:<br>

Finding the Red, Blue and Golden Keys so as to open the doors of respective colors. There are battery cells that you need to find to recharge your flashlight. Escape the facility and do not get hurt by 'Them'. If you encounter them, all you can do is **run**.


Do not, under any circumstance, give up.

# Good Luck Out There!
